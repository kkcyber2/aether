    How it is built

    Terrain

    A radial profile gives the basin its shape: resurgent dome, steep wall, rim crest, outer apron. Ridged fractal noise is masked to the wall so the plain stays readable, and a breach is cut into the rim where meltwater leaves.

    Surface

    Rock, scree, snow and ash are chosen per vertex from height and slope, so a cliff face is rock while the shelf above it holds snow. The lake sits at a fixed level and the floor is carved beneath it.

    Cost

    One draw call for the terrain, one for water, one instanced call for the treeline. No model files, no image textures — the whole world is arithmetic and a palette.
